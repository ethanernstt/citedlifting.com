Cited research, honest confidence

Every claim carries a real DOI or PMID, checked against Crossref and PubMed before it ships, and a label — strong, moderate, preliminary or contested — that says how much to trust it.

Programs from the evidence

Reps, effort, rest, frequency and ordering are read from the claims, not hardcoded. Change what the evidence says and the program changes with it.

A logger for the gym floor

Two taps a set, weight and reps prefilled from last time, a rest timer that survives leaving the app, and everything works with no signal.

Not all evidence is equal, and the label says so.

A prescription backed by three meta-analyses and one backed by a single eight-week study look different in Cited — on purpose. Where credible researchers disagree, the app says contested rather than picking a side.
