SUPPORT

How can we help?

Email support@citedlifting.com and a person will reply. Include the phone model and what you were doing when something went wrong, if that is what it is about.

Do I need an account?

No. Logging, programs and the evidence base all work on your phone with no account and no signal. An account only adds backup and sync across devices, and unlocks semantic search over the claims.

Subscriptions

Cited Pro is billed by Apple. Manage, change or cancel it in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions on your iPhone. A free trial becomes a paid subscription when it ends unless you cancel at least 24 hours before. New phone? Open the paywall and tap Restore purchases.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: Profile → Delete account, near the bottom, under Sign out (it shows when you are signed in). That removes the account and everything backed up to it immediately and permanently. Data on your phone stays until you delete the app. You can also email us and we will do it for you — no reason needed.

Something in a program looks wrong

Tap why? on the exercise. It shows the claims and studies that produced the number, and how confident the evidence is. If you think a citation is wrong, tell us — a mistaken citation is the one bug we treat as urgent.

Requesting a feature

We read everything. Nutrition tracking, cardio and social features are deliberately not on the roadmap; almost anything about programming, logging or the evidence base is fair game.
